Autopost Engine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Purpose

Autopost Engine is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's original video content to the owner's connected social-media accounts.

Information processed

The tool may process original media files, captions, scheduling data, platform account identifiers, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and delivery-status logs. It does not collect information from the public for sale, advertising, profiling, or analytics resale.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the owner's connected accounts, upload and publish the owner's selected content, monitor delivery status, and diagnose publishing errors.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials are stored in protected runtime configuration and are not placed in public source code. Access can be revoked by the owner through the relevant platform's security or application settings.

Sharing

Autopost Engine does not sell or share information with third parties. Content is sent only to social-media platforms selected and authorized by the owner for publication.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the social-media profile that published the relevant content.
